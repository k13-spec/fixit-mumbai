No action in two to three weeks?

  1. Reply on the same channel with your complaint number and a fresh photo of the same spot.
  2. Write to your ward's Assistant Commissioner and your corporator, quoting the number.
  3. File an RTI asking for the action taken on that complaint number.
  4. Escalate on the state grievance portal, grievances.maharashtra.gov.in, then the Lokayukta.

Fixit Mumbai is an independent citizen project. It is not affiliated with BMC, the police or any government body.
